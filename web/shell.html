LBA2 Options menu prototype

Your game files

The menu draws with the game's own font, picture and palette, so it needs files from your copy of Little Big Adventure 2: RESS.HQR, SCREEN.HQR and SPRITES.HQR (HOLOMAP.HQR too for the Twinsun background). Any copy with loose .HQR files works, the free 1997 demo included.

Choose the game's folder. The files stay in this browser (kept for your next visit); nothing is uploaded.

See About for what this is and its keys.

About

A fan-made prototype of a new Options menu for LBA2 Classic Community. It is not the game, and not affiliated with the game's owners. Settings are pretend: nothing is saved.

Esc goes back, arrows move, Enter picks. A gamepad works too. The menu's screen is the size of this window; resize the window to try other sizes.

The prototype's own keys

  • F2 / Shift+F2: a pretend screen size from lba2cc's list (shown with black bars); resizing the window goes back to the window's size
  • F1 overlay, F3 band, F4 typing, F8 help at 640x480 — switches being tried
  • 1–0 and Shift+them: more switches being tried (see the README)

Some browsers keep keys such as F5 (reload), F11 and F12 for themselves.
