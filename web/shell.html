LBA2 Options menu prototype

A fan-made prototype of a new Options menu for LBA2 Classic Community. It is not the game, and not affiliated with the game's owners.

The menu draws with the game's own font, picture and palette, so it needs files from your copy of Little Big Adventure 2: RESS.HQR, SCREEN.HQR and SPRITES.HQR (HOLOMAP.HQR too for the Twinsun background). Any copy works, the free 1997 demo included.

Choose the game's folder. The files stay in this browser (kept for your next visit); nothing is uploaded.

Click the picture first so it takes the keys. Esc goes back, arrows move, Enter picks. A gamepad works too.

The prototype's own keys
  • F2 / Shift+F2: the next or previous pretend screen size
  • F1 overlay, F3 band, F4 typing, F8 help at 640x480 — switches being tried
  • 1–0 and Shift+them: more switches being tried (see the README)

Some browsers keep keys such as F5 (reload), F11 and F12 for themselves.
