LBA2 Options menu prototype

Your game files

The menu draws with the game's own font, picture and palette, so it needs four files from your copy of Little Big Adventure 2:

RESS.HQRSCREEN.HQRSPRITES.HQRHOLOMAP.HQR

Any copy with loose .HQR files works, the free 1997 demo too. They stay in this browser for your next visit. Nothing is uploaded.

What this is, and its keys: About.

About

A fan-made prototype of a new Options menu for LBA2 Classic Community. It is not the game, and not affiliated with the game's owners. The settings are pretend: nothing is saved.

The menu's screen is the size of this window: resize the window to try other sizes, or step through lba2cc's list with F2, shown with black bars. The switches are described in the README.

UpDownMove EnterChoose EscQBack F2Screen size F1F3F4F8Switches being tried 1–0More switches

A gamepad works too. Some browsers keep F5, F11 and F12 for themselves.

In fullscreen, Firefox and Safari keep Esc for leaving it: use Q to go back. In Chrome and Edge, Esc goes back and holding Esc leaves fullscreen.
