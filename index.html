LBA2 menu prototype

Your game files

The menu draws with the game's own font, picture and palette, so it needs three files from your copy of Little Big Adventure 2:

RESS.HQRSCREEN.HQRHOLOMAP.HQR

Any copy with loose .HQR files works, the free 1997 demo too. They stay in this browser for your next visit. Nothing is uploaded.

What this is, and its keys: About. What the menu promises: How it works.

How it works

Seven things this menu promises. Each one says how to see it.

  1. Nothing is lost by accident. A row that throws your settings or your game away asks first, and the question starts on No. Try it: Resume game, Esc, then New game. Or Options, Preset, choose Classic, then Undo preset.
  2. Back is always one press. Esc leaves any page, whichever row you are on. On a pad it is B; in a browser, Q works too.
  3. Every setting explains itself. Its help appears as you move onto it, in the game's dialogue voice. A greyed-out row says why. Rows that say what they do, such as New game, need no help. Try it: Options, Controls and camera, set Outdoors camera to Classic, then move onto Camera speed.
  4. Classic looks like 1997. At 640x480 the menu looks as the 1997 one does, and the Classic preset brings back the 1997 picture and font. Try it: press F2 until the size reads 640x480, then choose Options, Preset, Classic.
  5. Keyboard and pad are equal. The key list at the bottom uses your pad's button names, and no key binding can lock the pad out of the menu.
  6. One language for the whole menu. Changing Text changes every word, the help included. Try it: Options, Language and interface, Text.
  7. The picture is never stretched. It is scaled evenly to fill the screen and cropped at the edges, and the menu grows in whole steps. Try it: resize the window, or press F2.

About

A fan-made prototype of a new game menu and Options for LBA2 Classic Community. It is not the game, and not affiliated with the game's owners. The settings and the saves are pretend: nothing is written, and no game is played.

The menu's screen is the size of this window: resize the window to try other sizes, or step through lba2cc's list with F2, shown with black bars. The prototype's keys are listed in the README.

UpDownMove EnterChoose EscQBack F2Screen size F1The prototype's keys

A gamepad works too. Some browsers keep F5, F11 and F12 for themselves.

In fullscreen, Firefox and Safari keep Esc for leaving it: use Q to go back. In Chrome and Edge, Esc goes back and holding Esc leaves fullscreen.
